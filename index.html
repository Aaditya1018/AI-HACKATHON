⚠️ Data Limitations & Transparency

Honest assessment of model constraints and dataset assumptions

🌡️

No Real Temperature Data

Temperature is approximated via a sinusoidal proxy based on month and hour. Actual weather measurements would significantly improve accuracy, especially during summer HVAC peaks.

🏢

Residential ≠ Commercial

The dataset originates from residential apartments. Campus buildings may differ—demand driven by lecture schedules, labs, servers, and events creates different consumption patterns.

📊

Missing Data Imputation

Significant data gaps exist due to smart meter outages. Linear interpolation fills these gaps, potentially smoothing out real consumption spikes or troughs.

🚪

No Occupancy Sensor Data

Occupancy is inferred from time-of-day heuristics (weekday 8am–10pm). Real occupancy sensors or room booking systems would significantly improve demand prediction.

⚙️

Model Does NOT Control Equipment

This AI system is a decision-support tool only. It identifies high-consumption periods and recommends practical checks. No direct connection to HVAC, lighting, or other systems.

📅

Temporal Distribution Shift

The model trains on Dec 2016–Apr 2018 data. Accuracy may degrade over time as occupancy patterns, appliance mix, and climate change cause distributional drift.
